# Build checklist (Kingdom mod buildings)

Collected from written building guides (WesterosCraft building fundamentals, medieval building guides) and vanilla village structures.

## Shape
- [ ] Depth: no flat walls. Frame (logs/beams) sits one step in front of the wall fill, or the fill is set back.
- [ ] One-block-out rule: foundation wider than the walls, roof wider than the walls.
- [ ] Roof has an eave/overhang on all sides (slabs or stairs), upside-down stairs as trim under the overhang.
- [ ] Steep roofs for medieval/Nordic (snow and rain), stairs for slopes, slabs for the edges.
- [ ] Some asymmetry: not everything mirrored around the center.

## Material
- [ ] No long unbroken runs of one block; mix blocks of similar color but different texture.
- [ ] Gradient: darker/rougher at the bottom (mossy cobble, cobble), lighter at the top.
- [ ] Contrast in moderation: textures next to each other must not clash.

## Detail (last)
- [ ] Windows: trapdoor shutters, stair or slab sills, flower boxes where it fits.
- [ ] Lighting as part of the build (lanterns on posts/hooks), not random torches.
- [ ] Interior: stairs as chairs, slabs/trapdoors as tables, barrels, chests.
- [ ] Ground around the building: path blocks, coarse dirt, gravel mixed, not a clean edge.

## Kingdom rules
- [ ] Vanilla blocks only.
- [ ] Kingdom color: banners/flags + at most one small accent (bed, single carpet or awning). Glass, walls, roofs stay natural.
- [ ] Style matches the category's historical model (Temperate: Central European half-timbered; Frozen: Norse).
